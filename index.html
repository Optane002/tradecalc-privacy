TradeCalc Privacy Policy

Effective 31 October 2026

Summary

TradeCalc is a calculator app for builders and electricians. It has no accounts and does not send your calculations, saved estimates or material prices to us. The app shows ads from Google AdMob, and Google may collect some device data to do that.

Data stored on your phone

Your settings, material rates, favourite and recent calculators, and saved estimates are stored only on your phone. We never receive them. If Android backup is turned on for your phone, Android may include this data in your personal Google backup so it can be restored on a new phone. Uninstalling the app deletes it from the phone.

When you use Share, the estimate text goes only to the app you choose (for example WhatsApp or email).

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The free version shows ads provided by Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, Google may collect your device's advertising ID, IP address, and information about your device and how you interact with ads. Google may use this to show personalised ads, where the law allows and you have agreed to it.

If you are in the European Economic Area, the UK or Switzerland, the app asks for your consent before showing personalised ads. You can change your choice at any time in Settings → Privacy choices.

You can reset your advertising ID or opt out of personalised ads in your phone's Android settings under Google → Ads. Read more in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

Purchases (Google Play)

The optional “Remove ads” purchase is processed by Google Play. We never see your payment details. The app only asks Google Play whether your Google account owns the purchase, so it can remove ads on any phone signed in to that account.

Children

TradeCalc is intended for adults working in the trades. It is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from them.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
